# Code of conduct

!!! info "Summary"
    This page summarises the [Spanish original](../../../participar/codigo-de-conducta/), which is the reference text. It is an adaptation of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) 2.1, whose [official translations](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/translations) you can also read.

## Our commitment

Participating in the Nervi community must be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We commit to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

What helps: empathy and kindness, respect for different opinions and experiences, giving and accepting constructive feedback, taking responsibility and apologising for our mistakes, and caring about what is best for the whole community.

What is not acceptable: sexualised language or imagery and unwelcome sexual attention; trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks; harassment in public or private; publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission; other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and in public spaces where someone represents the project. Community leaders may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow it, and will explain their moderation decisions when appropriate.

To report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour, write to **jerobareiro@gmail.com** (Nervi's lead maintainer). All reports are reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the privacy and safety of whoever reports an incident is respected.

## Consequences

Community leaders follow four steps, from lightest to most serious:

1. **Correction:** a private written warning explaining why the behaviour was unacceptable; a public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour; no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban:** exclusion from any public interaction with the community for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban:** permanent exclusion from any public interaction with the project community, for a sustained pattern of violations.
