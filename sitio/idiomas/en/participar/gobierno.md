# Nervi governance

!!! info "Translation"
    This is a translation of the [Spanish original](../../../participar/gobierno/), which is the reference text.

This document says who decides what and how to take on more responsibility in the project. It is reviewed as the community grows.

## Roles

| Role | Who | What they can do |
|---|---|---|
| Contributor | Anyone who sends an issue, a PR or a review | Propose changes and give opinions in discussions. |
| Technical reviewer | Engineers or teachers invited by the maintainers | Approve code-based modules and verification cases in their specialty. |
| Maintainer | People with write permission on the repository (list below) | Review and merge PRs, label issues, publish releases. |
| Project lead | Jerónimo Bareiro | Breaks ties, sets the roadmap and holds the Nervi trademark. |

### Current maintainers

- Jerónimo Bareiro (project lead)

## How decisions are made

1. **By consensus** in the issue or PR. Most changes are merged with one maintainer's approval and green CI.
2. **Design modules based on a code**: besides a maintainer, they need the approval of a technical reviewer experienced in that code. Until then they are published marked as experimental.
3. **High-impact changes** (license, sign convention, data contract, roadmap, adding maintainers): discussed in an open issue for at least 7 days and decided by the project lead, listening to the maintainers.
4. If there is no consensus, the project lead decides and writes down the reason in the issue.

## How to become a maintainer or reviewer

- **Maintainer:** sustained, quality contributions over a few months, and a proposal from a current maintainer. Approved with no objections from the other maintainers.
- **Technical reviewer:** training and experience in the area (a civil engineering or related degree, teaching or professional practice). Any maintainer can propose them.
- Roles are given up voluntarily or after more than 12 months of inactivity, and are recorded in this file.

## Institutions

Universities, departments and research groups can take part like any contributor. If they want a formal agreement (for example, a collaboration agreement or one for use in class), they should write to the project lead. An institution's contributions follow the same rules as everyone else's and keep the GPL-3.0-or-later license.

## Towards a technical committee

When there are at least three maintainers from two different institutions, the intention is to move high-impact decisions to a technical committee that votes by majority, and to consider a non-profit association or foundation as the holder of the trademark.

## Trademark

The code is free, but the name and the logo of Nervi identify the project. You can use them to talk about the project, in classes, publications and presentations. If you distribute a modified version, give it another name or describe it as "based on Nervi", so that nobody confuses your version with the official one.
