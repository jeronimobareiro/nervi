# Contribute

We build Nervi together: students, teachers, researchers and engineers from any country. You do not need to know Rust to contribute, and you do not need to speak Spanish either: **reports and proposals in English, Portuguese or Russian are welcome** and will be answered gladly.

## Ways to contribute

- **Report a bug.** If a result looks wrong to you, open an [issue](https://github.com/jeronimobareiro/nervi/issues/new/choose) with the smallest model that reproduces it and the value you expected, with its source.
- **Add a verification case.** A worked example from a textbook or an analytical solution is a valuable contribution even if you never touch the solver: it is a text file.
- **Implement a feature.** Look for [issues](https://github.com/jeronimobareiro/nervi/issues) labelled `buen-primer-aporte` (small, well-defined tasks), `proyecto-academico` (course-project sized, such as a final project or internship) or `tesis-posible` (topics for a thesis).
- **Improve or translate the documentation.** Examples, notebooks and translations help a lot.
- **Review a code-based module.** If you are a structural engineer or a teacher, your review is what lets a design module stop being experimental.

## Before you start

- The code, the comments and the technical documentation are written in **Spanish**. To read them, the [glossary](https://github.com/jeronimobareiro/nervi/blob/main/docs/glosario.md) gives the English, Portuguese and Russian equivalent of each term.
- Conversation (issues, pull requests and reviews) can be in **Spanish, English, Portuguese or Russian**.
- Every commit carries a Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO): add it with `git commit -s`. It is explained in [How to contribute](contribuir.md).
- Every contribution follows the [Code of conduct](codigo-de-conducta.md). The decision rules are in [Project governance](gobierno.md).
- A short introduction to the project in English is in the [README](https://github.com/jeronimobareiro/nervi/blob/main/README.en.md).
